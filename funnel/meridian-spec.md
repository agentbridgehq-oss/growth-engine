# Meridian Funnel Spec

## Offer
AI receptionist: answers calls, books jobs, never calls in sick.
$10,000/year target = 100 clients at $10k, or mix of $199/$499/$999 tiers.

## Landing Page Structure
- Hero: front desk headline + subhead + CTA
- Problem cards: missed calls, cold leads, no-shows
- Solution cards: 2-second answer, qualifies + books + confirms, revenue log
- How it works: 3 steps
- Pricing: 3 tiers, decoy architecture, Rule of 100
- Social proof: 3 one-line quotes (HVAC, dental, law)
- FAQ: 4 questions
- Final CTA: free 2-week trial, no card

## DM Script (Stan AutoDM)
DM1: Deliver demo link immediately.
Capture: Ask for consented email while 24h window is open.
Stop: Nurture by email after window closes.

## Email Flows
Welcome (5): Day 0 deliver+orient, Day 2-3 teach, Day 5 proof, Day 7 value, Day 10-14 soft pitch.
Cart recovery (3): 45min reminder, 24h objection, 72h incentive (high-value only).

## Kill Thresholds
Spam >0.1%, unsubscribes >0.5%/send, welcome opens <40%.
