# 30-Day Sprint

## Week 1 — Offer + Proof
- Name one buyer, one outcome
- Set value-based price + anchor
- Create one precise lead magnet
- Write guarantee and fit criteria

## Week 2 — Handoffs
- Wire Stan AutoDM on Instagram
- Deliver asset in DM #1
- Capture email with consent
- Test full mobile checkout

## Week 3 — Creative Tests
- Script 4 core lessons
- Cut 3 hooks for each
- Publish natively to 3 surfaces
- Keep winners by retention + shares

## Week 4 — Recovery + Scale
- Launch 5-email welcome flow
- Launch 3-email cart flow
- Review five-metric scoreboard
- Fix the weakest handoff only

## Paid Traffic Gate
Do NOT turn on $10-20/day at day 30.
Turn it on only when: one organic creative repeatedly produces leads and sales,
checkout is verified on mobile, and follow-up is live.
